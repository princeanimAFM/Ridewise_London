# TikTok business account: handoff

Paste this into the business session and say "continue from this handoff".

## The idea
A TikTok business account that wins app and website clients by showing common business problems and how a
custom app or web app fixes them. Each video speaks to one type of business owner ("that's me").
Do NOT use the AFM HUB app, Church Care, the pastor, or any real client in the videos. Use demo apps for
made-up businesses (e.g. "Kofi's Shipping", "Ama's Fashion House") with sample data, marked "Demo".

## Video format (20–40 seconds, vertical)
1. Hook in the first 2 seconds, on screen and spoken, e.g. "Do you run a shipping business and spend your whole day chasing payments?"
2. The pain (5–8s): WhatsApp chaos, "where's my parcel?", notebooks, transfer screenshots.
3. The fix (15–20s): screen recording of a simple demo app for that business.
4. Call to action: "Want one built for your business? Tap the link in my bio or WhatsApp me."

## First 12 video ideas (common in Ghana)
| Business | Headache | What the app does |
|---|---|---|
| Shipping / freight | Chasing payments, "where's my parcel?" | Tracking link per parcel, automatic updates, payment reminders |
| Clothing brand / tailor | Re-posting every item on Status | Online catalogue shared once; customers order with size |
| Restaurant / food vendor | Orders by voice note, mistakes | Menu link, neat orders, sold-out items hidden |
| Salon / barber | Everyone arrives at once, no-shows | Booking page with time slots and reminders |
| Landlord / rentals | Forgetting who paid rent | Tenant list, due dates, reminders, receipts |
| School / lesson teacher | Fees owed, results sent one by one | Parents' page for fees and results |
| Perfume / cosmetics | Price lists as images, "how much?" | Catalogue with prices and order button |
| Event planner | Guest lists on paper | RSVP page with QR check-in |
| Car rental | Who has which car, deposits | Bookings calendar and contracts |
| Pharmacy / provision shop | Running out of stock unnoticed | Stock list with low-stock alerts |
| Photographer | Sending photos one by one | Private gallery link per client |
| Cleaning / delivery riders | Not knowing who's where | Job list and status updates |

## Account setup
- Name: own name + "builds apps", or a brand name; use the same name on WhatsApp and the website.
- Bio draft: "I build apps and websites that save Ghanaian businesses time 📲 · DM 'APP' or WhatsApp ⬇️"
- Switch to a TikTok Business account: analytics, and a website link without needing 1,000 followers.
  Note: business accounts can only use TikTok's Commercial Music Library, not trending songs.
- Link in bio: one simple page with services, demo videos and a WhatsApp button (the existing portfolio,
  princeanimafm.github.io/prince-asamoah-anim, could be adapted).

## Money
TikTok creator payouts only run in a few countries and Ghana isn't one, so income comes from clients who
message, not views. Aim for business owners rather than a huge general following.

## Posting plan
- 3 videos a week, 7–9 PM.
- Reply to comments with short video replies ("Can it work for a pharmacy?").
- Every 4th video: "day in my life building an app" for trust.

## What Claude can produce per video
- Build a demo app for that business (sample data only).
- Record the screen walkthrough in phone format with captions (Playwright recording + ffmpeg, as done for the
  Church Care videos).
- Write the hook and script for the owner to read on camera or as a voice-over.
