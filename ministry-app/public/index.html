About Prophet Micah Felix Azanduna (D.D)

Prophet Micah Felix Azanduna (D.D) is a teaching prophet, philanthropist, philosopher, theologian and apologist. He is the founder of the Alleluia Faith Mission Global Assembly and the AFM Family Network.

He is the author of The Maker of Men, 1001 Scriptures: Genesis – Revelation, and The Voice of Honor: Honour and Dishonour. A transformologist, he has mentored thousands of people all over the world, and he continues to change lives by teaching God’s word through his life and his rare ability to make complex things very simple to understand.

He is also the CEO and founder of the House of Azanduna perfume brand.

Also known as The Brand AFM and Rabbi Micah. Teaching Prophet · Philanthropist · Philosopher · Theologian · Apologist · Author · Transformologist.
