1. Summary

[Client Organisation] wants a mobile app that brings its [messages, events, resources, products] together in one place, keeps members informed with announcements and newsletters, and makes it easy to [give / buy / book / get in touch].

This proposal covers building the app, setting up the systems behind it, publishing it on the Google Play Store [and Apple App Store], training your team, and supporting it after launch.

Why this matters: open with the client's goals in their own words. They buy outcomes (members informed, more giving, easy access) more than features.

2. What the app will do

For members and the public

  • Home screen with the latest content and announcements
  • Audio and video: stream messages in the app, with background play and lock-screen controls
  • Live streaming of services or events from YouTube
  • Daily quotes or devotionals, easy to share
  • Store: books and products with buy links
  • Giving: PayPal, Mobile Money and bank details with one-tap copy
  • About, leadership biography, handbook or FAQs
  • Social media and contact links, prayer or contact requests
  • Optional accounts: email sign-up, password reset
  • Newsletter sign-up by email or text
  • Push notifications for announcements and daily messages
  • AI assistant that answers questions about the organisation

For your team (admin dashboard in the app)

  • Post announcements with a flyer, and send them by email, text and push notification in one tap
  • "Write it with AI" to draft announcements from a few notes
  • Edit books, products, quotes, giving details, links, service times and the theme, with no app update needed
  • Add or remove admins
  • Subscriber counts and delivery results

Also included

  • App icon, store graphics and screenshots
  • Privacy policy and Play Store data-safety forms
  • Store listing text
  • Handover guide and a training session

8. Ownership, accounts and privacy

  • Your accounts: the store, backend, email and domain accounts are created in [Client Organisation]'s name. [Your name] is added as a team member for the build and support.
  • Code and content: when paid in full, you own the app's content and receive the source code. [Your name] may reuse general, non-client-specific code and may show the app in their portfolio.
  • Your data: member and subscriber data belongs to you, is stored in your accounts, and is only used to run the app.
  • Changes in scope: new requests after sign-off are quoted in writing before work starts.
  • Store approval: we follow Google's and Apple's rules and handle any review feedback. Final approval decisions rest with the stores.
